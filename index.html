How can we help?

Bandwise is speaking and writing practice for IELTS: answer the way the test asks, and get an estimated band and the sentences to fix.

Contact

Send us a message. Say which phone you use and what happened; a screenshot helps. We reply within two working days.

Common questions

Is the band my real score?
No. Every band in Bandwise is an estimate from an AI examiner, marked against the public band descriptors, and it can be half a band or more out either way; the likely range is shown with it. Pronunciation is judged from the recording itself, never from a transcript.
How do I cancel or change my subscription?
Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Bandwise. Cancel at least a day before a renewal or the end of a free trial and you pay nothing more.
I subscribed on another phone.
Open the plans in Bandwise and tap Restore. Bandwise Pro follows your Apple ID.
An answer was not marked.
It is saved on your phone. Open Today or All answers, tap it, and mark it again. It is not counted until it is marked.
How do I delete my data?
In Bandwise, open Settings and choose “Delete everything on this phone”. Nothing about you is held on our side; the privacy policy says exactly what is sent and when.
